Why AI subscriptions pile up so quietly

It almost never happens in one big decision. A chatbot joins in January because everyone is talking about it. A writing tool gets added when a deadline bites. An image generator appears for a single project, a voice tool for a single video — and each $10 to $40 charge feels too small to think about. None of them ever sends you one honest yearly bill. They just renew, quietly, in the background.

The real problem is rarely the total. It is overlap. Two writing assistants with the same features. A chatbot subscription alongside a writing tool that already includes one. Paid tiers bought for limits you never reach, while the free tier would have done the job. Individually these are rounding errors; together they can add up to several times a streaming bill — money that buys you nothing extra.

How to audit your AI stack in five minutes

  1. Tick everything above — including the trials you meant to cancel. If you pay for it, it counts.
  2. Read the yearly figure, not the monthly one. $39 a month feels painless; $468 a year makes you think.
  3. Cut overlaps before cutting tools you love. Keep the highest-rated option in each category and drop its understudies.
  4. Check free tiers honestly. If a tool's free plan covers your actual usage, the paid tier is a donation.
  5. Re-run this audit every few months. New tools launch every week — we track and review them — and yesterday's essential subscription is often today's duplicate.

That is all this page asks of you: two honest minutes now, instead of twelve quiet renewals you never chose.

AI subscription costs — common questions

How much does the average person spend on AI tools?

There is no single official average, because stacks vary so much. A common creator setup — one AI chatbot, one writing tool and one image generator — runs roughly $50–$90 a month at entry-level prices. Many people also pay for two or three tools that do the same job without realising it, which is exactly what this calculator is built to surface.

Are the prices in this calculator exact?

They are the typical entry paid plans taken from our own listings, which we base on each tool's public pricing. Plans change often, so treat every figure as a close estimate and confirm the current price on the tool's official site before you buy — each tool above links to its full review with pricing notes.

What is the fastest way to cut my AI bill?

Drop overlaps first: keep the highest-rated tool in each category and cancel the rest. Next, check whether a free tier already covers your real usage — many people pay for limits they never hit. Finally, compare monthly against annual billing before you renew, since many tools discount the yearly plan.

Do you store which tools I tick?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser — nothing you tick is sent to us, stored, or tracked. Close the tab and your selections are gone.
