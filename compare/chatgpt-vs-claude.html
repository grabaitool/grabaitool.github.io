ChatGPT vs Claude

ChatGPT vs Claude is the defining AI assistant contest: OpenAI's everything-platform against Anthropic's careful reasoner. Both write, code, analyse documents and search the web — the differences live in emphasis, ecosystems and how their free tiers behave at busy hours.

This page puts the two side by side using the same verified facts as our full reviews: pricing models, entry prices, feature lists and honest pros and cons. No benchmark theatre — just what changes your day-to-day work.

Side by side

ChatGPT

★★★★★ 4.9/5 editorial · Freemium · from ~$8/mo

The conversational AI assistant for writing, code, and ideas.

  • Conversational answers across writing, code, and analysis
  • Voice mode for spoken conversations
  • Image and document understanding
  • Custom GPTs for specialized tasks
  • Memory of preferences across conversations

Best for: One assistant for everything — chat, images, voice, deep research and the biggest ecosystem of apps and custom GPTs.

Read our full ChatGPT review →

Claude

★★★★★ 4.8/5 editorial · Freemium · $20/mo

A thoughtful AI assistant built for careful, long-form work.

  • Large context window for long documents
  • Projects with files for ongoing work
  • Strong coding assistance and integrations
  • Careful, well-formatted responses
  • Voice and file analysis capabilities

Best for: Long documents, careful writing and coding judgement — anywhere the quality of the reasoning matters more than the feature count.

Read our full Claude review →

Pricing compared

ChatGPT (Freemium): Free plan available; Go from ~$8/mo, Plus ~$20/mo — check the official site for current pricing.

Claude (Freemium): Free plan with usage caps. Pro is $20/mo billed monthly ($17/mo billed annually); Max plans from $100/mo add much higher limits — check the official site for current pricing.

Where ChatGPT wins

  • Versatile across a huge range of tasks
  • Strong ecosystem of integrations and plugins
  • Free tier is genuinely capable

Where Claude wins

  • Excellent at editing and long-form writing
  • Handles very long documents reliably
  • Responses feel measured and well-reasoned

The honest downsides

ChatGPT:

  • Can confidently produce wrong answers
  • Usage limits push heavy users to paid plans
  • Knowledge cutoff means dated facts on current events

Claude:

  • Free tier usage caps are fairly tight
  • Occasionally over-cautious with refusals
  • Smaller third-party ecosystem than ChatGPT

The verdict

If you can only pay for one, ChatGPT is the safer default: it does more things in one subscription, and its free tier is the one most people already know. Choose Claude when your work is dominated by long documents, serious writing or code review — its judgement in those lanes is why its users stay.

Plenty of professionals quietly run both: ChatGPT for breadth and images, Claude for the draft that has to be right. Our full reviews break down each one's pricing and limits in detail.
