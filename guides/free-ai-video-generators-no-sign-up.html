Free AI Video Generators — No Sign Up: The Honest Shortlist (2026)

"Free AI video generator, no sign up" is one of the most-searched phrases in AI video — people want a clip, not another account. Here is the honest version: unlike AI images, almost no quality video generator works with literally zero sign-up, because video costs real compute per second. What does exist is one genuinely account-free route (open models on your own machine) and a strong set of free-credit plans that start within a minute. Both are below, labelled plainly.

Truly no account: run it yourself

1. Wan 2.2 (open weights)

Wan is the one honest answer to "no sign up": Alibaba released the Wan models up to version 2.2 as open weights, so you download the model once and generate on your own computer — no account, no credits, no watermark, no queue. The trade-off is hardware: you need a capable GPU and some patience with setup. Alibaba's hosted Wan 2.5 is the polished version (from $0.05/sec via its API, with a 50-second free quota for new accounts), but the free, private, unlimited route is the local one.

Free credits with a quick sign-up (worth the minute)

2. Kling 4.0

Kling 4.0 is the model to test first: new accounts start with free credits, and the model generates native 30-second clips with audio in a single pass — the longest mainstream generation right now. Paid plans start from $8.80/mo when the credits stop being enough.

3. Dreamina

Dreamina, ByteDance's studio (by the CapCut team), gives free accounts daily credits — 120 a day for US users at the time of writing — across its Seedance video and Seedream image models. Daily-refilling credits make it the best free option for regular experimenting. Paid memberships start at $15/mo; note that free-plan output is for personal, non-commercial use.

4. Luma Dream Machine

Luma Dream Machine is the easiest video model to learn: natural motion, keyframes and camera controls, with free generations to learn on before any plan is needed.

5. Pika

Pika is built for short, playful clips and effects — squish, inflate, melt — with a free tier that suits social experiments better than client work.

6. Hailuo AI (MiniMax)

Hailuo is unusually good at character performance and emotional acting, and offers free credits to test whether its style fits your scene.

7. PixVerse

PixVerse is the value pick of the free-credit crowd: frequent free credits, templates and effects aimed at social output.

What "no watermark" and "unlimited" claims really mean

Read free video plans the way editors do. "Free" almost always means: a starter bundle of credits, a watermark or resolution cap on free exports, and commercial rights that only begin on paid plans. Credits are spent per second and per resolution — a "generous" credit bundle can be three 1080p clips. And "unlimited" plans elsewhere in this market usually mean unlimited generations at the lowest quality tier, in a slow queue. None of this makes the free tiers useless — they are exactly right for testing a model on your own prompt before paying anyone. Just test with the clip you actually need to make.

Which one should you start with?

No account at all and a decent GPU: Wan 2.2 locally. The best free first impression: Kling 4.0's starter credits. Something fresh to test every day: Dreamina's daily credits.
