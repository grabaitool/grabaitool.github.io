Kling AI Alternatives: AI Video Generators Compared

Kling 4.0 is formidable — native 30-second clips with native audio — but it meters hard: 10 credits per second at 720p and 15 per second at 1080p, and the free plan excludes 4.0 generations entirely. Most alternative-hunting starts when the credits burn faster than the ideas, or when a project needs a different strength: camera control, character consistency, playful effects.

Below are the video generators in our directory that genuinely compete, with their free routes and entry prices verified. Our full Kling 4.0 review covers the credit maths in detail.

Free tiers to test before you pay

Every tool here lets you generate real clips free — expect watermarks and small credit allowances. Test the same prompt on each; motion quality varies more between models than any spec sheet admits.

How to choose

Compare cost per finished second, not plan price: Kling's 10–15 credits a second, Pika's monthly credits and Runway's credit packs all meter differently. Generate your hardest shot — fast motion, hands, on-screen text — on each free tier, and let that one clip, not the demo reel, pick your tool.
