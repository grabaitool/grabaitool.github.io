ElevenLabs Alternatives: Free Tiers, Trials & Paid Voice Tools

ElevenLabs is the name most people start with for AI voices — and its free tier is real, with paid plans from ~$6/mo that undercut most rivals. So an alternative usually wins on shape, not price: listening instead of generating, singing instead of speaking, cloning your own voice, or editing audio like a document.

One honesty note before the list: unlimited free text-to-speech barely exists. What the tools below offer is free plans, free tiers and trials — enough to judge voice quality on your own script before any money moves.

Free plans and trials to test first

Run the same 200-word script through each free tier. Voice differences you cannot hear in a polished demo become obvious on your own words.

How to choose

Test on your own script, in your own language — voice tools vary wildly by language, and the demo reel is always the best-case take. If your need is straightforward narration, ElevenLabs' own ~$6/mo Starter is hard to beat on price; switch when a rival's workflow (Descript), catalogue (Speechify) or cloning control (Resemble AI) matches the job better.
