Otter.ai Alternatives: 6 Meeting Transcription Tools Compared (2026)

Otter.ai made the AI notetaker normal. People switch for price at team scale, for a notetaker that doesn't announce itself as a bot, or for sales features — CRM sync, deal insights, coaching — that Otter treats as add-ons.

The first block covers the direct rivals; the second covers the different philosophies: invisible, sales-first and privacy-first.

Direct rivals

Bot joins, transcript and summary out — the Otter workflow, at different prices and depths.

Fireflies.ai

★★★★☆ 4.5/5 editorial · Freemium · from ~$18/mo

AI notetaker that records and summarizes every call.

Fireflies is the team-scale Otter rival: conversation intelligence, CRM sync and a searchable meeting library.

Read our Fireflies.ai review →

Fathom

★★★★★ 4.6/5 editorial · Freemium · $16 per user/mo

Free AI meeting notes with instant summaries and highlights

Fathom's free plan is famously generous — unlimited recordings and basic summaries that make it the budget default.

Read our Fathom review →

tl;dv

★★★★☆ 4.4/5 editorial · Freemium · from $18 per user/mo

Meeting recorder that turns calls into clips and insights

tl;dv turns calls into clips and highlights — the research and sales-call sharing favourite, strong on free features.

Read our tl;dv review →

Different philosophies

Bot-free notes, sales intelligence and privacy-led recording.

Granola

★★★★★ 4.7/5 editorial · Freemium · from $14 per user/mo

AI notepad that transcribes meetings without a bot

Granola takes notes without a bot joining at all — it enhances your own notes from your device's audio.

Read our Granola review →

Read AI

★★★★☆ 4.2/5 editorial · Freemium · from $15 per user/mo

Meeting reports with engagement scores, plus search everywhere

Read AI adds engagement and sentiment analytics to transcripts — how the meeting landed, not just what was said.

Read our Read AI review →

MeetGeek

★★★★☆ 4.1/5 editorial · Freemium · from $15 per user/mo

AI meeting assistant with highlights, tasks and follow-ups

MeetGeek focuses on automated summaries and follow-ups pushed to your CRM and tools.

Read our MeetGeek review →

How to choose

Our steer: Fathom is the free trial everyone should run first. Choose Granola if the bot itself is the problem, Fireflies if the meeting library becomes company infrastructure.

Every tool above is listed in our directory with verified pricing and an editor rating. Start with the full Otter.ai review to see exactly what you are switching from, or put two finalists head to head in the comparison tool.
