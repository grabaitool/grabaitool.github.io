Midjourney Alternatives: Free Options First

Midjourney still sets the bar for artistic AI images, but it is paid-only — plans start at ~$10/mo, there is no free tier, and generation lives inside its own app. Most people searching for an alternative want one of three things: a free way in, text that actually renders correctly inside images, or output cleared for commercial work.

The shortlist below is ordered free-first, using the free tiers and credits our editors verified in each listing. Ratings and prices come from the tools' directory pages, which we re-check regularly.

Free Midjourney alternatives (start here)

Free here means a real free tier, free credits or open source — expect queues, watermarks or daily caps rather than unlimited generation.

Stable Diffusion

★★★★☆ 4.0/5 editorial · Free · Free

Open-source image model you can run and modify.

The truly free route: open-source, runs on your own hardware, no account and no per-image meter. The cost is setup effort and a capable GPU.

Read our Stable Diffusion review →

Ideogram

★★★★★ 4.6/5 editorial · Freemium · from $8/mo

Text-to-image generator famous for accurate text inside images

The text-in-image champion — posters, logos and memes with the words spelled right. Free tier with a small daily allowance; paid plans from $8/mo.

Read our Ideogram review →

Leonardo AI

★★★★☆ 4.2/5 editorial · Freemium · from ~$12/mo

AI image studio for game art, characters, and design.

Free daily tokens and a proper studio feel, aimed at game art, characters and design work. Paid plans from ~$12/mo.

Read our Leonardo AI review →

Adobe Firefly

★★★★☆ 4.3/5 editorial · Freemium · from ~$10/mo

Adobe's commercially-safe AI image and video generator.

The commercially-safe pick: trained on licensed content, with a free plan carrying monthly credits and deep Adobe app integration. Paid plans from ~$10/mo.

Read our Adobe Firefly review →

Recraft

★★★★☆ 4.5/5 editorial · Freemium · from $10/mo

Design-grade image generator with vector and brand-style control

Design-grade output with vector support and brand-style control — the alternative designers pick. Free plan with daily credits; paid from $10/mo.

Read our Recraft review →

Craiyon

★★★★☆ 3.5/5 editorial · Freemium · from $5/mo

The original viral DALL-E mini — free, fun AI drawing for everyone

Zero-friction fun: the original DALL-E mini, free in the browser with ads and slower queues. Supporter plans from $5/mo remove ads and speed things up.

Read our Craiyon review →

Worth paying for

When the free tiers run out, these two earn their money in different ways — one by already sitting inside your chatbot subscription, the other by editing as well as generating.

DALL-E 3

★★★★☆ 4.5/5 editorial · Freemium · ~$20/mo

OpenAI's image generator built into ChatGPT.

Already paying for ChatGPT? DALL-E 3 is built in, with strong prompt-following and no second subscription. Free tier with limits; Plus ~$20/mo.

Read our DALL-E 3 review →

Krea 3

★★★★☆ 4.3/5 editorial · Freemium · Freemium

Fast AI image-editing model that keeps products, people, and text intact while changing the rest.

An editor as much as a generator: it changes products, people and text inside images while keeping them intact. Free tier with compute units.

Read our Krea 3 review →

How to choose

If budget is the whole question, Stable Diffusion (free, local) and Ideogram's free tier cover most casual needs. If the images earn money, judge on commercial terms and workflow instead: Firefly for licensed safety, Recraft for brand control — and know that Midjourney itself still wins on raw artistry, so sometimes the alternative search ends with the original, on the cheapest tier that fits.
