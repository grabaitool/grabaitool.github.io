InVideo AI Alternatives: 6 AI Video Makers Compared (2026)

InVideo AI's promise — type an idea, get a complete video with stock footage, voiceover and music — is now a whole category. Rivals differ in what they optimise: articles, faceless YouTube, or giving you a real editor afterwards.

The first block covers the same prompt-to-video job; the second covers adjacent workflows people often actually need.

Prompt and article to video

The direct rivals: scripts and articles in, finished videos out.

Pictory

★★★★☆ 4.1/5 editorial · Freemium · from ~$29/mo

Turn scripts and articles into shareable videos.

Pictory is the article-to-video specialist — blog posts become stocked, captioned videos built for content marketing.

Read our Pictory review →

Fliki

★★★★☆ 4.1/5 editorial · Freemium · Freemium

Turn ideas and scripts into videos with AI voices and avatars

Fliki pairs a huge AI voice library with stock video — the faceless-channel workhorse.

Read our Fliki review →

Lumen5

★★★★☆ 4.0/5 editorial · Freemium · from ~$29/mo

Turn blog posts into social videos automatically.

Lumen5 is the simplest repurposer: paste a link, get a social video in your brand kit.

Read our Lumen5 review →

Editor-first and social-first

When you want to keep cutting after the AI drafts, or the video starts from your own footage.

Kapwing

★★★★☆ 4.3/5 editorial · Freemium · Freemium

Browser-based video editor with AI subtitles, dubbing, and repurposing

Kapwing gives you a real browser editor around the AI features — better when the draft needs human hands.

VEED

★★★★☆ 4.4/5 editorial · Freemium · Freemium

Edit, subtitle, and publish social videos entirely online

VEED adds avatars, eye-contact correction and team review on top of generation.

Read our VEED review →

Steve AI

★★★★☆ 3.8/5 editorial · Freemium · Freemium

Create animated and live-action videos from text or audio

Steve.AI covers animated and live-action explainer styles, popular for training and ads.

How to choose

Our steer: Pictory for content teams, Fliki for faceless channels, Lumen5 for the fastest link-to-video turnaround. If you keep re-editing InVideo's drafts elsewhere, Kapwing removes that round trip.
