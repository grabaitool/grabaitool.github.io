What is Surfer SEO?

Surfer SEO is a content optimization tool that scores your articles against top-ranking pages for a keyword. It gives writers a concrete checklist of terms, headings, and structure to cover.

It also includes AI writing assistance, keyword research, and content audits. The approach is data-driven: match what Google already rewards, rather than guessing.

Who Surfer SEO Is Best For

Best for: Marketers, founders, and growth teams get the most from Surfer SEO — makes on-page SEO actionable for non-experts; guidelines are grounded in real ranking data.

Not ideal for: Think carefully if these limits affect your work: price is steep for solo bloggers and startups; following scores too closely can produce formulaic content.

Key Features

  • Content editor with real-time on-page SEO score
  • SERP analyzer comparing your page to top competitors
  • AI writer for drafting optimized articles
  • Keyword research with search intent clustering
  • Site audits and internal linking suggestions
