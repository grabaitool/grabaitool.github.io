What is Lumen5?

Lumen5 turns blog posts, articles, and scripts into social-media videos with stock visuals, music, and animated text, aimed at small marketing teams and non-designers. The pitch is that video creation should be as easy as writing.

Its templates are formatted for different platforms, and a brand kit feature keeps colors and logos consistent. A free plan exists for casual use, with watermarked output on lower tiers.

Key Features

  • Article-to-video from a link or pasted text
  • Library of stock photos, video clips, and music
  • Platform-specific templates for social feeds and stories
  • Brand kit with custom fonts, colors, and logos
  • Talking-head and scene-based video layouts

Pros

  • Genuinely easy for non-designers to make passable videos
  • Good for quickly repurposing blog content
  • Brand kits keep team output looking consistent

Cons

  • Free and basic plans watermark or limit output quality
  • Videos can look template-driven and generic
  • Paid plans are pricey for what is largely stock assembly

Pricing

  • Model: Freemium
  • Free plan available; paid plans from ~$29/mo — check the official site for current pricing.
