What is Speechify?

Speechify is a text-to-speech reader for students, professionals, and anyone who prefers listening over reading. It reads web pages, documents, books, and PDFs aloud.

You import or scan text, pick a voice and speed, and listen on mobile or desktop while you do other things. It is popular as a reading companion for coursework and long articles.

Key Features

  • Reads web pages, PDFs, emails, and scanned documents
  • Large selection of AI voices in many languages
  • Adjustable playback speed up to several times normal
  • Mobile apps for listening on the go
  • Highlighting that tracks the text as it reads

Pros

  • Genuinely useful for heavy readers and students
  • Handles scanned documents and photos of text
  • Good voice selection even on the free plan

Cons

  • Premium pricing feels high for a reader app
  • Occasional mispronunciations in dense material
  • Sync quirks across devices for some users

Pricing

  • Model: Freemium
  • Free plan available; Premium from ~$12/mo — check the official site for current pricing.
