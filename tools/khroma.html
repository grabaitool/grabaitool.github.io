What is Khroma?

Khroma is a free AI color tool for designers. You pick fifty colors you like, and it trains a personal algorithm that generates palettes matched to your taste.

It also shows how generated palettes look on real typography, imagery, and gradients, and checks contrast ratios for accessibility. It is a handy companion for branding and UI work.

Key Features

  • Personal color algorithm trained from your chosen colors
  • Unlimited palette generation in chosen styles and hues
  • Live previews on type, gradients, and imagery
  • Built-in contrast and accessibility checking
  • One-click export of palettes to design tools

Pros

  • Completely free with no account traps
  • Personalized results beat generic palette generators
  • Accessibility checks built into the workflow

Cons

  • Single-purpose tool, not a full design workflow
  • Training step takes a few minutes upfront
  • Palette variety depends on your training picks
