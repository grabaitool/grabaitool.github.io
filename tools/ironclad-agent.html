What is Ironclad Agent?

Ironclad Agent is the conversational front door to Ironclad's contract platform, launched alongside a Contract Knowledge Graph that maps clauses, relationships, obligations, and past negotiation decisions across a company's agreements. The point is grounding: recommendations are meant to come from your prior deals, counterparty positions, and internal benchmarks rather than generic model training alone.

It is aimed at legal, procurement, and sales teams that need to ask questions across contracts, orchestrate specialized agents, and move through negotiations, renewals, and risk reviews with a clearer audit trail. A policy-based access control model replaces hundreds of one-off permissions with shared policies, so regional or specialist teams only see the contracts relevant to them.

Key Features

  • Conversational interface for asking questions across contracts and workflows
  • Contract Knowledge Graph connecting clauses, relationships, obligations, and past decisions
  • Recommendations grounded in prior deals, negotiation history, and internal benchmarks
  • Orchestrates specialized AI agents for legal, procurement, and sales work
  • Policy-Based Access Control that scales permissions by shared criteria instead of one-off settings

Pros

  • Uses a company's own commercial context instead of treating every contract as isolated text
  • Preserves institutional deal knowledge even after negotiators move on
  • Access policies are designed to stay auditable as teams and regions grow

Cons

  • Quote-only enterprise pricing makes quick cost comparison difficult
  • Full value depends on having your contract history and workflows inside Ironclad
  • Legal teams will still want human review before acting on agent recommendations
