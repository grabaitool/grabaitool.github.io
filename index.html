How we find the best AI tools

New AI tools launch every week, and most of them blur together. Grab AI Tool exists to cut through that noise. Our editors research each tool hands-on: what it actually does, who it is really for, what it costs, and where it falls short. We publish the findings in plain English — features, honest pros and cons, and a clear verdict.

Every tool on this site is scored on four criteria: ease of use, feature depth, value for money, and output quality. Scores reflect our editors’ independent judgment at the time of review. AI products change fast, so we revisit popular tools regularly — and we always note that pricing is approximate, so verify current plans on the official site before you buy.
